    Our Kia PV5 camper

    Kia PV5 Passenger Elite · Skyline pop-top · Campal Camp Box

    Our new family car, which we are turning into a fully capable family camper van. Here is the build step by step, the gear we use and answers to common questions.

    The build

    From a stock van to a family camper, in the order it happened. Every step has its episode: press the button to watch it here.

    1. 1Day one

      Straight to the workshop

      After weeks of waiting for the delivery day, our brand-new Kia PV5 Passenger Elite came home, and it did not stay stock for long. In late June 2026, straight after we picked it up, it went to a workshop: paint protection film (PPF), window tint, interior protection film, and goodbye Kia badges. Huge thanks to @kasparfolie.

      Watch EVF#02

    2. 2Gear

      A fridge, and a scratch

      Our fridge is the Outwell Arctic Chill 41 Dual Zone. We found it the hard way: we scratched our brand-new van while trying another one.

      Watch EVF#03

    3. 3Roof

      The Skyline pop-top roof, fitted in England

      In July 2026 we drove over 1 000 miles from the Czech Republic to England, through the Channel Tunnel, to Skyline Roofs in Bristol. There they cut a huge hole in the roof of our brand-new van and fitted a Skyline pop-top roof. It made ours the first Kia PV5 in continental Europe with a Skyline pop-top.

      The Campal Camp Box

      After Skyline, our next stop was Campal, where the camper interior conversion began. We install the Campal Camp Box together with window curtains and privacy blinds, and the Camp Box transforms into a bed.

    Questions

    Which van do you drive?

    A new Kia PV5 Passenger Elite – our family car, and our small camper too. Step by step we adapt it to exactly what we need for travelling and for our comfort. And everything we change and try on it, we show on our YouTube channel.

    How many people travel in it?

    There are four of us – the two of us and our two children. Sometimes our four-legged darling Elvis comes along too. Because we are a big family, we wanted a van that is not only a camper but one we can also use in everyday life. And so far we enjoy finding out how much we can fit into it.

    What is the sleeping and storage setup?

    We have a Skyline pop-top roof, where two of us sleep comfortably. The Campal Camp Box turns into a bed for two more people in a moment, and it also folds out to serve as a dining area and a small kitchen. All our things are packed in bags and other gear from All In Van. We show the complete setup in our van tour (EVF #11).

    When do videos come out on YouTube?

    A new Electric Van Fam video comes out every Thursday at 8 pm Czech time, which is 7 pm in the UK. We mostly film our real experiences, family road trips, camping and the upgrades of our PV5 – no gloss and no marketing talk. The Electric Van Lab and Electric Van News formats come out now and then too – so follow us and don't miss a thing.

    Where else can I follow you?

    On Instagram we are @electric.van.fam, where we share behind-the-scenes moments, short videos and everyday news from our life with the PV5. On YouTube you find the full videos, tests, upgrades and our road trips. So if you enjoy electric vans and travelling as a family, you are in the right place.
